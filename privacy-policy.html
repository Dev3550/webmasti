Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Welcome to WebMasti. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal information that is received and collected by WebMasti and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

WebMasti operates as an open streaming portal. We do not require account registration or collect sensitive personal data such as your name, passwords, or banking information. We utilize local browser storage (LocalStorage) to store your watch preferences (such as your last played series and episode) locally on your device.

2. Log Files & Cookies

Like many other web applications, WebMasti makes use of standard log files and browser storage. The information inside the log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks to analyze trends and administer the site.

3. Third-Party Advertising Partners

WebMasti displays advertisements served by third-party ad networks (such as Monetag and Adsterra). These third-party ad servers or ad networks use technology to serve advertisements directly to your browser. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs.

  • Third-party ad networks may use cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons to measure the effectiveness of their advertisements and to personalize the advertising content that you see.
  • WebMasti has no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers.

4. DMCA & Copyright Compliance

WebMasti does not host or store copyrighted media files on its servers. All video links provided on WebMasti are indexed from publicly available third-party servers. If you believe your copyrighted content is linked on our platform, please contact us with proof of ownership for immediate removal.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please contact our support team.
